# Hill Valley County Appraiser's Office
## Policy 2 — Assessment Calendar and Statutory Deadlines

**Effective:** January 1, 2025
**Owner:** Office Administrator

> ⚠️ **Fictional policy and fictional dates, written for an IAAO conference
> demonstration.** Real deadlines are set by statute and vary by jurisdiction.

---

### 2.1 Assessment date

All property is assessed as it stood on **January 1** of the assessment year.
Condition, ownership, and use on that date govern for the entire year. A house
that burns down on January 2 is assessed as it stood on January 1.

---

### 2.2 The annual calendar

| Date | Event | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| **January 1** | Assessment date. Values lock to conditions on this day. | — |
| **January 15** | Deadline for property owners to file for a homestead exemption for the current year. | Taxpayer |
| **February 1** | Personal property renditions due from businesses. | Taxpayer |
| **March 15** | Prior year ratio study published under Policy 9.7. | Valuation |
| **April 1** | Deadline for agricultural classification applications. | Taxpayer |
| **May 1** | Preliminary values complete; internal review begins. | Valuation |
| **June 15** | **Notices of Proposed Value mailed to every owner.** | Administration |
| **July 25** | **Deadline to file an appeal.** 40 days from the notice mailing. | Taxpayer |
| **August 1 – September 30** | Informal review conferences held. | Valuation |
| **October 1 – October 31** | Board of Equalization hearings. | Board |
| **November 5** | Roll certified to the Tax Collector. No further changes for the year. | Chief Deputy |
| **November 15** | Tax bills mailed. | Tax Collector |
| **December 31** | Taxes due without penalty. | Taxpayer |
| **January 1 (following)** | Unpaid taxes become delinquent. Interest accrues at 1% per month. | Tax Collector |

---

### 2.3 The appeal deadline

**An appeal must be filed within 40 days of the date the Notice of Proposed
Value was mailed.** For the 2025 assessment year, notices were mailed June 15
and the deadline to file was **July 25, 2025**.

The deadline is jurisdictional. The office has no authority to accept a late
appeal, and neither does the Board of Equalization. The only exception is where
the office can confirm the notice was mailed to an incorrect address of record
through office error, in which case the 40 days runs from the date the owner
actually received notice.

Filing an appeal does not defer the tax. The undisputed portion of the bill is
due on the normal schedule under Section 2.2, and interest accrues on anything
unpaid after December 31 regardless of a pending appeal.

---

### 2.4 Informal review comes first

An owner who disagrees with a value is encouraged — but not required — to
request an informal review conference before filing a formal appeal. Most
disagreements are resolved there, usually because the office is holding
incorrect characteristic data: wrong square footage, a bathroom that was never
built, a finished basement that is not finished.

An informal conference does **not** extend the July 25 appeal deadline. An owner
whose conference is scheduled after that date must file the appeal first to
preserve it.

---

### 2.5 Correcting errors outside the appeal window

Clerical and characteristic errors may be corrected at any time, including after
the roll is certified, for the current year and the **three preceding years**.
This covers:

- Arithmetic and data entry errors
- Characteristics that do not match the property as it exists
- Duplicate assessment of the same parcel
- Property assessed that is not within the County

It does **not** cover a difference of opinion about value. A value dispute must
go through the appeal process in Section 2.3.

---

### 2.6 Splits and combinations

Applications to split or combine a parcel are governed by Policy 4. Note the
interaction with this calendar: an application approved after **November 1**
cannot take effect the following January 1, because the roll is certified
November 5 and cannot be reopened. It takes effect January 1 of the second
following year instead.
