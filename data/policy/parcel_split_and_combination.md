# Hill Valley County Appraiser's Office
## Policy 4 — Parcel Splits and Combinations

**Effective:** January 1, 2025
**Supersedes:** Policy 4, revised March 2021
**Owner:** Deputy Appraiser, Cadastral Services

> ⚠️ **This is a fictional policy for a fictional county.** It was written for
> an IAAO conference demonstration. It is modeled on common practice but is not
> the law anywhere. Do not use it as authority for any real determination.

---

### 4.1 Purpose

This policy governs how the Appraiser's Office reviews a request to divide one
parcel of record into two or more parcels (a **split**), or to merge two or more
contiguous parcels into one (a **combination**).

A split or combination changes the parcel of record. Because the parcel of
record is the unit against which taxes are levied and liens attach, no change
may be made without confirming the status of the existing parcel with the other
offices of the County.

---

### 4.2 Outstanding taxes bar a split

**No parcel split or combination shall be approved while any ad valorem tax
remains outstanding against the subject parcel for the current tax year or for
any prior tax year.**

This includes partial balances, accrued interest, and penalties. A payment plan
in good standing does **not** satisfy this requirement; the balance must be paid
in full.

The reason is practical. A tax lien attaches to the parcel, not to the owner.
When a parcel is divided, the County must apportion any existing lien across
the resulting parcels, and Hill Valley County has no mechanism to do so. A
split approved over an unpaid balance therefore creates an unenforceable lien
and the levy is lost.

**Verification is required, not assumed.** The requesting party's statement
that taxes are current is not sufficient. Staff shall obtain the balance
directly from the Tax Collector's system for the current year and for the two
preceding years.

---

### 4.3 Minimum lot size

Each parcel resulting from a split must independently satisfy the minimum lot
area for its zoning district:

| Zoning district | Minimum lot area | Typical neighborhoods |
|---|---|---|
| R-1 Single Family | 6,000 sq ft | Hilldale, Lyon Estates, Courthouse Square |
| R-1A Single Family, large lot | 12,000 sq ft | Twin Pines Ranch |
| R-2 Two Family | 7,500 sq ft | Eastwood Ravine |
| R-3 Multi Family | 10,000 sq ft | scattered |
| C-1 Commercial | 8,000 sq ft | Courthouse Square |

A parcel that would leave any resulting lot below the minimum shall be denied
regardless of the tax status.

---

### 4.4 Additional requirements

A complete split application includes all of the following:

1. A current survey prepared by a licensed surveyor, dated within 24 months.
2. A legal description for each resulting parcel.
3. Written confirmation from the Clerk of Courts that the applicant is the
   owner of record and that no lis pendens or unreleased mortgage encumbers the
   parcel in a way that would prevent division.
4. Confirmation of a zero tax balance under Section 4.2.
5. Verification of legal access for each resulting parcel. A resulting parcel
   with no frontage on a public right of way shall be denied.

---

### 4.5 Effective date of a split

An approved split takes effect on **January 1 of the year following approval**.
The existing parcel remains the unit of assessment and taxation for the whole
of the year in which the split is approved.

Applications are accepted year round. Applications approved after
**November 1** are held and take effect January 1 of the *second* following
year, because the roll is certified in early November and cannot be reopened.

---

### 4.6 Denial and appeal

A denial is issued in writing and states the specific section of this policy
relied upon. An applicant may correct the deficiency and reapply at any time;
there is no waiting period. A denial under Section 4.2 is cured by paying the
outstanding balance in full, after which the application may be reinstated
without a new filing fee if reinstated within 90 days.

---

### 4.7 Combinations

Combinations are reviewed under the same tax requirement in Section 4.2, applied
to **every** parcel being merged. All parcels must be contiguous, in identical
ownership, and within the same zoning district and taxing district.
